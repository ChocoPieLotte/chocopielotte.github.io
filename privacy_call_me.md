# Privacy Policy for CallMeNow

**Last updated: 25.09.2025**

## Overview
CallMeNow is a video calling application that allows users to make video calls with other users.

## Information We Collect

### Camera and Microphone Access
- **Purpose**: We access your camera and microphone to enable video calling functionality
- **Usage**: Camera and microphone data is used only for real-time video communication between call participants
- **Storage**: We do not record, store, or save any video or audio data from your calls
- **Sharing**: Video and audio data is transmitted directly between call participants and is not shared with third parties

### Network Information
- **Purpose**: We collect basic network information to establish and maintain video call connections
- **Usage**: This information is used solely for the technical operation of the video calling service

## Data Security
- All video and audio data is transmitted using secure protocols
- We do not store any personal data on our servers
- Video calls are peer-to-peer when possible

## Third-Party Services
This app uses Agora SDK for video calling functionality. Please refer to Agora's privacy policy for their data handling practices.

## Your Rights
- You can revoke camera and microphone permissions at any time through your device settings
- Revoking permissions will prevent the app from making video calls
